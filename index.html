Civic Watch

Remain calm. Report everything.

A small town. A federal grant. A heavily armed neighborhood watch. Civic Watch is a gun-comedy FPS set in Fairview, with fourteen arenas, zombie waves, and neighbors who have been waiting to speak.

Mecca Gecko

Paint. Hide. Get spotted.

A free hide-and-seek party game for iPhone and iPad. Paint yourself into the scenery, become a suspiciously cute prop, or grab a toy paint blaster and find your friends. Four maps and thirty-four characters come with the game.

  • Free on the App Store
  • iPhone and iPad
  • Rated 9+
  • Optional purchases
