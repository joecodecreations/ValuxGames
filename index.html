Four ways to play

Go solo with bots.
Or bring the group.

Pick a hiding style, pick a game type, and try to keep a straight face. While hiders hide, seekers play mini games.

  • Hide style

    Hide and Paint

    Sample colors and patterns, paint your character, then crouch, curl up, lie flat or cling to a wall.

  • Hide style

    Prop Hunt

    Copy an object in the map and tuck yourself among the real props. Seekers have to spot what doesn’t belong.

  • Game type

    Red vs Blue

    A team series that swaps hiding and seeking between rounds. Play it with Hide and Paint or Prop Hunt.

  • Game type New

    Infection

    Every hider who gets hit becomes a seeker. Survive to the buzzer, or infect everyone.

  • 01 / Roll

    A free roll every 60 seconds.

    Auto Roll keeps the dice moving. 15 rarities from Dirt, 1 in 2, up to Cosmic Mythic, 1 in 10,000,000. Luck potions tilt the odds, and the Odds panel shows every number.

  • 02 / Fight

    The Arena portal opens every 3 minutes.

    Knock rivals off the edge, parry the Echo Ball and double jump to survive. KO effects use your aura’s colours.

  • 03 / Win pets

    Every round is fought for a prize animal.

    23 to collect, from the Black Sheep to the Lava Dragon. The super-rare ones mean beating the Void Titan boss first.

  • 04 / Earn

    Pets earn cash every second.

    Carry your prize home to your yard, then spend on shields that block hits until they break.

  • 05 / Trade & flex

    Trade with anyone.

    Show off from the VIP glass balcony in the Hub, and watch the weekly limited aura rotate in every Friday.

    Cinematic slow-mo of your aura.

    One tap hides the UI and orbits the camera. Built for clips.
